Main navigation EN About Bitkom Services Publications New to Bitkom Become a member About Bitkom Services Publications New to Bitkom Become a member Position Paper How AI Is Transforming the Software Industry Software is increasingly becoming the operating system of our economy and society. "How AI Is Transforming the Software Industry" analyses the profound structural changes that AI is triggering in development processes, software products, value creation and business models, and describes the software industry’s distinctive dual role as both a driver and a beneficiary of this transformation. To ensure that the software industry remains a driver of economic growth in the future, the right course must be set today. "How AI Is Transforming the Software Industry" identifies specific areas for action in politics, business and education to secure the industry’s competitiveness, attract skilled workers and strengthen Germany’s role as a leading digital economy in the long term.
